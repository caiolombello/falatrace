## Selection rationale and measured colors

In the rendered side-by-side comparison at 16/24/32px, the new bracket and separate point remained distinct without color; the old F signal had a crowded lower branch/endpoint. This is visual inspection, not a recognition study or trademark clearance. The bracket also matches source-citation syntax and reduces the commit-graph association. The circular avatar has extra crop-safe padding; no part of the mark is clipped.

Mint and apricot do not alone distinguish semantic states. Source points use a circle plus a timestamp; frames use a square/image label; REC uses a separate red square and text. Light source text is #0F705A; light budget text is #A34125.
