# FalaTrace 0.2.0-alpha.19

Experimental Linux source release that keeps a meeting's text readable while its video is open, and fixes the colours, scroll bars and caption placement found in a review of the Studio at many window sizes. Everything else is alpha.15 to alpha.18, described in [ALPHA15.md](ALPHA15.md), [ALPHA16.md](ALPHA16.md), [ALPHA17.md](ALPHA17.md) and [ALPHA18.md](ALPHA18.md).

## Changes

**Room to read under the video.** A video meeting opens with the video above the notes, and the pane took up to 45 % of the whole meeting page, header included: at 1366×768 about 180 px were left for the notes, at 900×640 or a quarter of a 4K screen at 145 % (1324×708) little more than the summary paragraph. The pane is now sized from the area under the tabs: up to 55 % of it, but always leaving the document about 280 px (62 % in cinema), and never taller than the 16:9 picture. At 1366×768 the notes get about 280 px instead of 180. A divider under the video (`videoPaneDivider`) sets the height by hand for the session: drag it, or focus it and use Up and Down; a double click or Home goes back to the automatic height. Normal and cinema mode keep their own height, and the document always keeps at least 160 px.

**Cinema in tall windows.** Cinema mode put the notes beside the video whenever the window was at least 1100 px wide, so a tall half-screen window drew the picture in a narrow, very tall pane with black bands above and below. The notes go beside the video only when the room left for it is wider than tall (at least 1.1 times); otherwise the video and the notes stack.

**Captions and speakers follow the picture.** The caption was anchored to the bottom of the frame and the speaker chip to its top, so in a letterboxed frame (a tall pane, full screen, a video that is not 16:9) they sat in the black bands far from the picture. Both now follow where mpv draws the picture (`video-params/aspect`): the caption sits right under the picture when the band has room, otherwise over its lower edge, at most three or four lines (longer captions scroll) and no wider than 36 em (about 70 characters); its size follows the picture height. mpv is told not to draw its own subtitles or messages (`sub-auto=no`, `sid=no`, `osd-level=0`), so they cannot appear under the Studio's captions.

**Text on primary buttons.** The palette's `onAccent` colour never applied: QML reads a property named `on` + capital letter as a change handler of `accent`, so the text and icons of primary buttons (play, Gravar…, Assistir gravação, Continuar, Salvar…) and checked checkboxes painted black — 3.5:1 on the light theme's green. It is renamed `accentInk` everywhere and paints white on the light theme, dark on the dark one. Because the window palette's highlighted text now really is `accentInk`, combo box popups get their own palette, so the highlighted row stays readable in both themes; long choices end with an ellipsis and show in a tooltip.

**Scroll bars that show.** In the Basic style a scroll bar stays invisible until it is used, so a cut-off list or document looked complete. The notes, the transcript and speaker lists, the sidebar, Home, Actions, People, Clients, Ask, live notes and the pickers now show their bar whenever there is more to scroll.

**Smaller fixes.** The player clock is 13 px and shows hours (1:02:05, not 62:05), also in full screen. The CC switch is outlined when off instead of a filled grey that read as disabled, with its own focus ring. The "Sem cliente" chip is drawn at full opacity (it was 3.5:1 in the light theme), and the disabled quick-start card of the setup assistant no longer dims its description below 4.5:1.

## Verification

The Studio QML was rendered offscreen with PySide6 6.11 against a synthetic bridge and a painted stand-in video that reports a loaded 16:9 file: 766 scenes (17 states — 12 of a meeting, plus Home, Actions, People, Clients and Ask — at 19 window sizes from 900×640 to 2649×1416, and 15 dialogs at four sizes, in both themes), plus every meeting state at 1324×1416, 1324×708 and 2649×1416 at scale 1.45. A layout lint over every visible text item (clipping, text under the bars, overlaps, contrast against the nearest opaque background) went from 379 low-contrast texts to none, with no new clipping or overlap that is not content scrolled out of view; the remaining reports are lint false positives (clipped list content, the Ask drawer and full screen covering the page by design), checked one by one. An independent visual QA pass by a second model compared the scenes before and after and approved them with the caveats listed below. Pane heights, the divider's limits and its session memory, and open combo popups in both themes were checked in the same harness.

On the release machine (Fedora 44 KDE, Bun 1.4.0) the offline suite ran 2,594 passing and 34 failing tests across 248 files (32,663 assertions), the same failing set as an untouched alpha.18 copy on the same machine: all environment-only (its `node_modules` predates the Anthropic SDK dependency, FFmpeg lacks libx264 for the synthetic-video tests, and strace is not installed). For the same reason the type check and the desktop type check report the missing `@anthropic-ai/sdk` exactly as on alpha.18 (both pass in CI, which installs the dependencies fresh), and Biome reports 0 errors (587 warnings, as on alpha.18). All 18 Studio journeys pass (975 assertions across 207 screens), run with Qt logging sent to standard error, which Fedora's default `qtlogging.ini` hides; one journey (`video-ux`) caught a too-small default video height and a too-strict cinema rule during this work, both fixed before the release. Public CI runs the install, type checks, lint, offline suite, build and journeys on the pull request.

## Limits

The video in these checks is painted, not decoded: real mpv rendering through MpvQt and OpenGL, hardware decoding and fractional scaling on a real Wayland screen were not exercised, and readability on the reporter's monitor still has to be confirmed there. Keyboard use of the divider (Up, Down, Home) and drag were checked through the same functions the keys and the mouse call, not with real key presses. Everything listed in [ALPHA15.md](ALPHA15.md#limits) still applies.

## Installation and rollback

Same as alpha.18: update only when no capture or processing is active, and reconnect existing MCP sessions to use the new executable. Configurations, data and services from alpha.18 are unchanged; the divider height is kept only for the session. Alpha.18 remains available for rollback. This document describes the release, not an installed-state receipt.
