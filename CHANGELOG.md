## 0.2.0-alpha.16 — the Studio opens on Qt 6.10

- **Studio on Qt 6.10.** Alpha.15's Ask panel named a variable `short`, a word the Qt 6.10 QML engine (the system Qt of Ubuntu 26.04) refuses as an identifier, so the whole Studio failed to load with "Type AskPanel unavailable". The variable is renamed, and a new test refuses declarations and parameters in the Studio's QML and JavaScript named with any word Qt 6.10 reserves, each confirmed against libQt6Qml 6.10.2. The Studio journeys in CI run on PySide6 6.11, which accepts some of these words, so they could not catch it.
- **Checks.** See [ALPHA16.md](docs/ALPHA16.md).

## 0.2.0-alpha.15 — notes during the meeting, AI that completes them, and a redesigned Studio

- **Notes during the meeting.** A notepad saved per capture keeps the moment each line was typed, measured from the start of the recording; editing a line keeps its time, and two windows never overwrite each other. In the Studio the notepad is the page while you record, and **My notes** on the meeting page afterwards. Notes are private files on this computer. CLI: `notes show|edit|path`.
- **AI notes that complete yours, with citations.** Summaries now follow a template: your notes stay as your items under the section they fit, and AI items complete them with what was said, each citing transcript segments; uncertain items are marked, and notes the model did not use are listed instead of dropped. Unknown note or segment ids fail the summary. `summary.md` marks your items with `✎` and every item with the time of its first citation. Existing summaries are unchanged until regenerated.
- **Templates and summary language.** Nine built-in templates (general, 1:1, stand-up, planning, retrospective, sales discovery, incident postmortem, hiring interview, user research) and custom ones as JSON files next to the configuration, with an editor in Settings. Each meeting gets your choice, the default, a template matched from the calendar title, transcript keywords or note headings, or the model's pick in the same request. `summary.language` writes in the conversation's language (`auto`) or a fixed one. Regeneration from the reviewed transcript takes a template, a language and the notes, and saves the choice for that recording; like before, it runs only with Ollama on this computer. CLI: `templates list|show|init|delete`.
- **Ask your meetings.** Questions about one meeting, a person, a client or the whole library, answered only from excerpts and citing every sentence with markers checked against what was sent. A local library index (keyword search, plus meaning-based search through the local Ollama when `assistant.embeddingModel` is set) finds the passages. Providers: Ollama on this computer (default), API keys for OpenAI, OpenRouter, Anthropic (official SDK), Gemini, xAI, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, Together or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, ACP agents (Codex, Claude, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Kiro, Copilot, Cursor, goose, Qwen, Grok or a custom command) started in an empty folder with a minimal environment that holds no FalaTrace secrets and in their most restrictive mode, every tool permission refused, subscription CLIs run once per question (`claude`, `codex`, `gemini`, `opencode`) in read-only or plan modes; an agent that can still run tools of its own (all but goose and the `claude` CLI) answers only once you accepted that for it in Settings (`assistant.agentToolsAccepted`), and Sign in with ChatGPT. The destination is shown first; anything outside the computer needs consent, Ollama cloud models included. Conversations are kept locally. CLI: `ask`, `library refresh|search`, `assistant status|detect|models|login-chatgpt|logout-chatgpt`.
- **Recipes.** Ten built-in tasks for the assistant (follow-up email, risks, postmortem document, executive summary, next steps, Slack update, open questions, formal minutes, prepare my next meeting, what did I promise) and custom recipes with variables, with an editor in Settings. Meeting titles and names reach the model as data, never as part of the task. CLI: `recipes list|show|run`.
- **People, clients and actions.** People from confirmed speaker names, calendar guests and known voices, with rename, alias, merge and hide; clients from the timesheet catalog, editable with a backup, plus per-recording client choices; every action item (summary actions and unchecked note to-dos) with an open, done or dismissed status that survives regeneration. CLI: `people list|show|rename|merge|alias|hide`, `clients list|show|add|rename|delete|assign`, `actions list|done|reopen|dismiss`.
- **Reminders and a brief.** An optional user service notifies shortly before a Google or GNOME calendar meeting, with **Gravar** or **Pular esta gravação** depending on the recording mode, and up to three lines on what earlier meetings with the same people, series or client left open. A record or skip decision is honoured by the call monitor for that meeting's own call only: never for a call already going on, and a pause set later wins. Calendar ids are stored as salted hashes. CLI: `reminders status|test|install-service|uninstall-service`.
- **Live transcript.** An optional preview while recording, from a separate low-priority FFmpeg tap: Whisper.cpp on this computer, or OpenAI (about US$0.27/h) or Groq (about US$0.04/h) in chunks (prices checked 2026-10-25). With a provider outside this computer, turning it on is consent for every capture's audio, and turning it back on during a capture asks again for that provider. The lines are pruned 30 days after the capture and deleted with the recording. The final transcript is still made after the meeting. CLI: `live show`.
- **Share.** A meeting's notes as Markdown to the clipboard, an Obsidian vault (never overwriting your edits), a Notion page, a Slack webhook or an AI app (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini on the web, Claude Desktop through the clipboard, or a terminal with `claude`, `codex`, `gemini` or `opencode`), after a preview. Notion, Slack and AI apps need consent each time, bound to the destination the preview showed; a local log records the destination and content hash, never the content. CLI: `share <job-id> --to … [--include summary|summary+notes|full] [--dry-run] [--yes]`.
- **Media retention.** `media.retention` can keep only the audio or only the transcript once the transcript is verified, the jobs are settled, backups are complete and speaker work is done or `media.graceDays` passed. With the archive on, media the archive never registered is kept until `falatrace archive backfill` registers it. Notes, transcript, summaries, questions and sharing keep working; remote and backup copies are never touched. CLI: `media status|retention|remove`.
- **Faster first notes.** With local processing, `processing.fastPath` processes a recording as soon as it stops. A clickable "Notas prontas" notification opens the meeting in the Studio through `falatrace://` links (`studio open`). First-use milestones (setup, first recording, first summary) are recorded locally, never sent.
- **A redesigned Studio.** A sidebar with Home, Actions, People, Clients and the meetings grouped by day, with search through the transcripts. A meeting opens on its notes (your lines and the AI's, citations that play the moment, actions you can tick, the template chip and **Generate again…**), with the transcript, speakers and agent access on tabs and the player in a strip below. Starting a recording opens the notepad, with an optional live transcript beside it. Home lists the next meetings with their brief and record or skip choice, the open actions and the first-notes card. Ask opens beside any page (Ctrl+J), and **Send** shares after a preview. A shortcuts dialog (F1). Tooltips show plain text.
- **Watching the recording.** A video recording shows **Watch the recording** with a film tile and opens with the video expanded; an audio-only one shows **Listen to the recording**. Cinema mode puts the notes beside the video on a wide window, or below it; full screen (F or a double click) keeps the captions and the speaker overlay, and Esc leaves it first. Citations seek and keep the size.
- **Settings for the new features.** Six new sections (notes and summaries, Ask and recipes, calendar and reminders, live transcript, sharing, privacy and retention), sixteen in all, in three groups. Every provider or agent choice says in one line what leaves the computer and what it costs. Keys for the assistant presets, `NOTION_TOKEN` and `SLACK_WEBHOOK_URL` are saved write-only and tested with one read-only request. The reminders service can be applied and disabled there.
- **Setup quick start.** **Quick start (recommended)** goes straight to the review with audio only, processing on this computer, the fast path on and automatic recording off. **Download what is missing** starts the Whisper and Ollama downloads early, with their size and an estimated time; Finish waits for them and can finish by itself when they end. **Record a 1-minute test** records a short conversation that stops by itself and opens the meeting; Home shows how long the first notes took.
- **Speaker names.** After a diarization, the microphone track of FalaTrace's own captures suggests the account's name for the speaker heard there while the computer is quiet (local only). **Sugerir nomes…** in the Speakers tab can also send the diarizer's turns to the configured summary model, after showing where they go, to find names said in the conversation; a suggestion is kept only when its quote is a literal excerpt that contains the name. **Usar** prefills the review and **Aplicar sugestões** saves one human revision. CLI: `speakers suggest|queue|status`.
- **Known voices, on this computer.** With `speakers.voices` on and voice recognition downloaded from Models (about 48 MiB: the sherpa-onnx runtime and the 3D-Speaker ERes2Net model, both Apache-2.0, pinned by size and SHA-256 and never a project dependency), confirmed names teach a 512-number signature per person, and later recordings suggest "voz · 78% de semelhança" when a person is clearly closest. Signatures only, never audio; `falatrace voices` lists and deletes them. Off by default.
- **Google Calendar, directly.** With an OAuth desktop client of your own Google Cloud project (`google.clientId` and `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) and **Connect Google account** in Integrations (or `falatrace google connect`), a call's meeting title comes from Google before the GNOME calendar, and a conversation request also carries the names invited to the recording's event. PKCE on `127.0.0.1`; only the refresh token is kept, privately, and **Disconnect** revokes it. Scopes: `openid email calendar.events.owned.readonly`.
- **Simpler CLI help, in Portuguese.** `falatrace help` opens with a one-line pitch and lists commands by task (record, process, notes, ask, people and actions, share, configure, services, advanced); details moved to `falatrace help <command>`. Streamed answers are made terminal-safe.
- **Fixes from the final review.** Ollama cloud models count as leaving the computer (consent asked, no fast path, never labelled "neste computador"), also through a custom API on this computer; changing a custom ACP agent's arguments asks for consent again; the **Generate notes…** consent names the meeting notes it sends; Ask's consent counts the meetings instead of showing NaN; retention never deletes media the archive never registered, and a stale running regeneration no longer keeps media forever; a capture's notes stay reachable from its recording beyond the 2,000-file scan, and the capture's stop stores where its media ends; a fast-path job is no longer stranded by its unit's 90-minute limit; planning new notes no longer outlives the Studio's 30-second request limit; a recording at its 32-request limit says so; turning notes or live on in Settings takes effect without a restart; in the Studio, the template picker opens inside the window and lists are read again on each opening, actions that need removed media are off everywhere, window shortcuts leave the focused control its own keys, Ask's consent and problem cards stay in view with the focus, Tab leaves the notepad and other multi-line inputs, and the warning notice is readable in both themes.
- **Other fixes.** Local processing no longer waits forever for a call monitor that is not installed; deleting a job also deletes its calendar link, its live transcript, the conversations about it and its share log; a remote processing plan no longer claims to send the notes (a remote worker never receives them); a stopped legacy recording whose job could not be queued is returned with a warning instead of a failed stop (the alpha.14 known issue).
- **Docs.** A shorter README with real screenshots of the new Studio, and new guides: [notes and templates](docs/NOTES-AND-TEMPLATES.md), [assistant](docs/ASSISTANT.md), [people, clients and actions](docs/PEOPLE-CLIENTS-ACTIONS.md), [reminders and live transcript](docs/REMINDERS-AND-LIVE.md), [sharing](docs/SHARING.md), [privacy and data](docs/PRIVACY.md), [evidence and limits](docs/EVIDENCE-AND-LIMITS.md) and [development](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).
